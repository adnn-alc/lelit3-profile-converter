On the machine

Program coffee button 1 or 2 in the LCC menu: turn the profile on, then set each step's pressure and time.

    By hand with the paddle

    Open the paddle to start; the Lelit shows the shot timer. Steer by the gauge: the paddle % is only where to start.

      Why it won't be a perfect copy

      Meticulous and Decent machines can steer by flow and let the pressure follow the puck. The Lelit holds a target pressure (profiles) or a pump power (paddle) instead. This converter copies the shape and timing of the pressure curve, which is what you can see and control on the Lelit.

      Dial in with the grind first. If the gauge stays below the targets, grind finer. If it overshoots them or the shot runs fast, go coarser.
